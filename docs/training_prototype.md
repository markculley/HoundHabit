# The Guardian's Training Navigator: A Narrative

Think of this app as a **GPS for a dog's education**. It doesn't just tell a Guardian *what* to teach; it tells them exactly *when* to move forward and when to take a step back, ensuring that neither the human nor the dog becomes frustrated.

## 1. The Master Blueprint: Setting the Path

Before the first practice session begins, the Trainer (or a savvy Guardian) acts as the **Architect**. They design a custom roadmap for each behavior based on the dog's age and the Guardian's environment.

- **Building the Staircase:** Instead of one giant leap to a goal, the Trainer breaks a behavior into roughly **5 progressive steps**. For a puppy, these steps might be tiny to ensure constant "wins."
- **Consistency Over Time:** This setup acts as a **Memory Bank**. It "freezes" the training exactly where it was left off. If a Guardian returns to a behavior after a long break, the app remembers the last successful step, preventing them from pushing the dog too fast.

## 2. The "One Change" Rule

In this system, every behavior is broken down into small, manageable steps where the Guardian only ever changes **one** variable at a time:

- **Distance:** How far they are from the dog.
- **Duration:** How long the dog must hold the behavior.
- **Distraction:** Whether a treat is hidden in a hand or placed openly on the floor.

If the distraction becomes harder, the distance and duration remain easy. The Guardian never moves two "dials" at the same time.

## 3. The Traffic Light System

Every time they practice, the Guardian performs a set of **5 repetitions**. Based on the dog's performance, the app provides a "Traffic Light" signal to determine the next move:

- **Green (5/5):** The dog has mastered the task. The app suggests "leveling up" to the next step.
- **Yellow or Orange (2/5 to 4/5):** The dog is learning but isn't quite ready for a promotion. They stay on the current step to build confidence.
- **Red (0/5 or 1/5):** The task was too difficult. The app gently suggests dropping back to the **previous successful step** (e.g., from Step 3 back to Step 2) so the dog can experience a "win" again.

## 4. Finding the "Why"

The app also functions as a **digital journal**. After each session, the Guardian can jot down a quick note — such as *"Right before dinner"* or *"Neighbor was mowing the lawn."* Over time, they might notice a pattern: the dog is a "Green Light" student in the morning but a "Red Light" student in the evening. This helps them realize the dog might simply be tired at the end of the day, rather than the training failing.

## 5. The Invisible Coach

To keep the team on track, the app acts as a coach in their pocket. It sends a nudge to remind them to practice, and once they start, it runs a timer. This ensures the Guardian doesn't over-train, keeping the sessions short, fun, and successful for the dog.
